# Selection Record

Formal inclusion requires: (1) an official main-conference or official Findings record; (2) a multimodal task; and (3) token/block selection, sparse attention, visibility control, mask/kernel lowering, or distributed sparse attention as a core method contribution.

Adjacent examples retained for audit but excluded from counts: MoPE-CLIP (structured parameter/module pruning) and FastVLM (efficient vision encoder). ELIP is retained as arXiv-only. Workshops, withdrawn OpenReview submissions, pure vision sparse attention, generic quantization, generic MoE and parameter-only pruning are excluded.
