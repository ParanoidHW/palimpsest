while masked baselines converge quickly but plateau early,
XDLM sustains steady improvement throughout training,
demonstrating superior long-term scaling.
2. Preliminary
Discrete Diffusion Models (DDMs) are latent generative
models defined by two Markov processes: a forward pro-
cess and a reverse process. Considering a single categorical
variable x0, represented as a one-hot vector in {0, 1}N,
where N = |V| denotes the vocabulary size. Let zt rep-
resent the latent state at time t ∈[0, 1], with z0 := x0.
The forward process q progressively corrupts the data via
transition kernels q(zt | zs) for s < t, and the backward
process pθ(zs | zt) parameterized by θ iteratively denoises
the latent states to recover the clean data x0.
