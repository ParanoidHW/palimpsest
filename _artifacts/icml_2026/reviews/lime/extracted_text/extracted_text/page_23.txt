D.7. Relative Threshold (Ours)
We select all experts whose probability is at least a fraction θ of the maximum probability.
Sθ(x) =

i : wi(x) ≥θ · w[1](x)
	
where w[1](x) = maxj wj(x).
Analysis of selected expert count. Let ri(x) = wi(x)/w[1](x) ∈(0, 1] be the relative probability of expert i. Then:
|Sθ(x)| = |{i : ri(x) ≥θ}|
For a peaked distribution where w[1](x) ≫w[2](x), we have r[2](x) ≪1, so only the top expert is selected when θ > r[2](x).
For a flat distribution where w[1](x) ≈w[2](x) ≈· · · , we have ri(x) ≈1 for all i, so most experts are selected.
Why Relative Threshold: (i) Scale-invariant across different E. (ii) Single hyperparameter θ. (iii) O(E) computation. (iv) Interpretable:
θ = 0.5 means “at least half as good as the best.” (v) Guarantees at least one expert selected.
