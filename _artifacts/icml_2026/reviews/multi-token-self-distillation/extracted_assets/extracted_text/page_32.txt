Figure 14: The performance of our (Left) L3.1-8B-Magpie based MTP LM using the main
settings, equivalent to left of Figure 4 and Algorithm 1, (Middle) the “Offline Teacher”
variant of the objective, and (Right) the “GT Suprv.” variant evaluated on the GSM8K
benchmark after ∼100k steps of training. These settings are described in Section C.11 and
performance is tabulated in Tables 4 and 6. Performance tradeoff is visualized by plotting
the effective k value or “Acceleration Factor” versus the Accuracy on the benchmark; axes
are shared to make lateral comparison possible. While the performance of the two offline
configurations are comparable to the main online setting up to effective k of about ∼2, at
larger values of k the online method is far superior. For example, there is a 20% acc. gap
at static k = 5 between the online and GT Suprv.
